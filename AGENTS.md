## Profile
- Purpose: Misty Mahjong, the owner's own calm Mahjong solitaire for Android: an offline web game in a WebView shell, with its own art, music and sounds
- Stage: prototype (repository standard 2026-10-18)
- Gate: `npm test` for the game logic; the APK is checked by hand on a device (CI job `gate`: none yet)
- Landing: direct to main after a green `npm test`, a local build and a check on a device; agents land: yes
- Release: none; agents release: no
- Outside issues and PRs: agents triage; outside PRs merge: owner approves
- Sensitive paths: none

## Map
- `web/` is the game, plain ES modules without a build step. `index.html` plus `css/app.css` hold the screens; `js/main.js` wires everything.
  - Model, free of rendering and tested under node: `js/layout.js` (turtle positions in half-tile units), `js/tiles.js` (kinds, matching), `js/rules.js` (free tiles, available matches), `js/deal.js` (solvable deals by simulated play, solvable reshuffles with a fallback), `js/scoring.js`, `js/game.js` (state machine, timer, events), `js/rng.js` (seeded PRNG).
  - View: `js/render/tileart.js` draws the 42 faces as canvas paths and brush-font glyphs into a symbol layer and hands body, symbols, finish and back to the set's material; sprites are cached per set, kind and size (`flat` ones for the flip). `js/tilesets.js` is the data of the five selectable sets (id, German name, material, face palette, glow and dim colours); `js/render/materials.js` holds the five material renderers (pearl, jade, sea glass, porcelain, lacquer), all procedural: gradients, seeded value noise, canvas paths, no images. `js/render/board.js` lays out, draws and animates the board, turns matched pairs over in a strip-projected 3D flip and collects them as backs in the tray (`#collect`, a HUD element whose rect the board reads); `js/render/fx.js` holds petals and score labels; `js/audio.js` plays the music loop and effects through Web Audio; `js/settings.js` persists settings (`tileset` and `track` among them) and the high score in localStorage; `js/backgrounds.js` reads the background manifest; `js/i18n.js` has the German texts.
  - Assets: `assets/backgrounds/index.json` lists the selectable paintings (id, German name, file, thumb, `mat` tint, `accent`); adding or replacing a painting means editing that manifest and dropping the files next to it, no code change. `assets/audio/` holds the selectable Ogg Opus music loops, `music.json` listing them (id, German name, file, exact `loopSeconds` for sample-accurate looping; the first is the default, only the chosen one is decoded), and the effects. `assets/fonts/` holds subset woff2 fonts with their OFL texts. `assets/logo.svg` is the mark used on the title screen and as the source of the launcher icon.
- `tools/make_audio.py` turns the recordings in `tools/music/` into the music loops (`TRACKS` there): "Mondlicht auf dem Teich", the default, already an exact 28-bar loop that is only resampled circularly, and the owner's koto and erhu recording (17 bars at 100 bpm, its fade-out folded onto the start); it builds every effect from the CC0 instrument samples in `tools/samples/vcsl/` (sources in `SOURCES.md` there), retuning each note into the music's D-flat major pentatonic (numpy, scipy, ffmpeg for decoding, Opus and loudness). A chain of matches plays `chain2` to `chain6`, one file per level, rather than a sped-up sound, so effects never leave the key. `tools/paint_backgrounds.py` renders the ink-wash backgrounds procedurally (numpy, PIL). Both are deterministic; rerun them after changing them and commit the results.
- `tests/` are node tests (`npm test`): layout, rules, dealing, reshuffling, scoring, the game state machine and the tile-set manifest.
- `android/` holds the manifest, resources (adaptive launcher icon as vector drawables plus a legacy PNG) and `MainActivity`, a full-screen WebView that serves `assets/game/` under `https://appassets.androidplatform.net/` and answers every other host with 404. The app has no INTERNET permission; VIBRATE powers the optional haptics.
- `scripts/build-apk` stages `web/` into `build/assets/game/`, then builds and signs `build/misty-mahjong.apk` with aapt2, javac, d8, zipalign and apksigner. No Gradle.
- `THIRD-PARTY.md` lists every third-party asset and library with its licence. Everything else is original work.

## Rules
- Nothing from any other Mahjong game may come in: no code, art, layout file, font or sound, and no transcription of its music. All assets are original or under a permissive licence, recorded in `THIRD-PARTY.md`.
- The game stays offline and private: no network requests, no analytics, no INTERNET permission, no URL parameters. The WebView serves only `assets/game/`.
- The calm character comes first. Effects stay small, slow and translucent; nothing flashes, shakes the screen or plays loudly. The board must stay readable over every background: keep paintings mid-to-dark and calm in the middle.
- Every tile set shows the traditional faces: dots, bamboo (bird on the one), characters, winds, three dragons, four flowers and four seasons with their numbers, and the red, green and blue colour coding stays recognizable. A set may change the material, never the symbols. Backs and materials are original work; nothing is traced from any product's tiles. Faces are drawn once per size into the sprite cache, never shaded per frame; a new material must keep the 42-face warm-up well under a frame budget's worth of frames (about 70 ms on the test phone today).
- The music loops sample-accurately: `audio.js` loops the decoded buffer between 0 and the track's `loopSeconds` from `music.json`, so never trim or re-encode a `music-*.ogg` by hand; regenerate them with `tools/make_audio.py`. Its Ogg files differ byte-wise on every run (random stream serial) while the decoded audio stays identical, so commit only the files whose sound changed.
- Keep `targetSdkVersion` below 36: from API 36, large screens ignore the landscape lock. The layout degrades gracefully in portrait, but the game is designed for landscape.
- d8 from build-tools 34.0.0 crashes on anonymous classes compiled by JDK 21 javac (it cannot read their `MethodParameters` entry). Use named static nested classes in `android/src`.
- Never commit `build/` or an APK.

## Checking a change
Run `npm test`, then `scripts/build-apk`. To check the web part in a desktop browser, serve `web/` (`python3 -m http.server --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory web`) and open `/index.html`; headless Chromium with device emulation gives phone and tablet screenshots, and `window.mistyMahjong` (the app) exposes `applyTileSet(id)`, `newGame()`, `game.tap(i)` and `view` for scripted captures. After a change to the tile art, render every set's 42 faces plus back at phone size and look at them. For the app, install `build/misty-mahjong.apk`, start a game and check that the music plays and choosing the other track in the settings crossfades to it, a tile pair matches and flies to the tray, switching the set in the settings redraws the running game, rotation keeps the game, and the timer stops while the app is in the background. `scripts/build-apk --debug` gives a build whose WebView answers on the `webview_devtools_remote_<pid>` socket for frame timing.
