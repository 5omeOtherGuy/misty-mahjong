How to play

Tap two identical free tiles to remove them from the board.

Any two flowers match, as do any two seasons.

A tile is free when no tile lies on top of it and its left or right side is open.

Each pair scores its suit value times the number of tiles still on the board. Chains in the same suit multiply the points; a hint breaks the chain.

The ten-minute timer starts with the first pair. Clear the board to earn the remaining time as a bonus. When no pairs remain, the garden reshuffles the tiles.
